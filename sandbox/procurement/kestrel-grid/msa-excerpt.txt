SECTION 9 — SECURITY OBLIGATIONS

9.1 Incident Notification. Supplier shall notify Customer's designated security
contact of any Security Incident affecting the products or services provided
under this Agreement, and which poses a cyber security risk to Customer, no
later than twenty-four (24) hours after Supplier becomes aware of such Security
Incident.

9.2 Incident Response Coordination. Following notification under Section 9.1,
Supplier shall designate a named incident response lead, shall participate in
joint response calls at Customer's request, and shall cooperate with Customer's
incident response process including the provision of forensic artifacts within
Supplier's control.

9.3 Vulnerability Disclosure. Supplier shall disclose to Customer all known
vulnerabilities affecting the products or services provided under this
Agreement, including vulnerabilities identified in third-party or open source
components incorporated therein, within thirty (30) days of Supplier becoming
aware of such vulnerability, or immediately where the vulnerability is being
actively exploited.

9.4 Software Integrity and Authenticity. For each release of software, Firmware
or patch supplied under this Agreement, Supplier shall publish a cryptographic
signature generated with Supplier's code signing key, together with a SHA-256
digest of the released artifact, and shall make the corresponding public key
available to Customer through a channel independent of the artifact
distribution channel. Supplier shall confirm the public key fingerprint to
Customer upon request.

9.5 Key Rotation. Supplier shall notify Customer not less than thirty (30) days
prior to any planned rotation of a code signing key, and immediately upon any
compromise or revocation of such key.

SECTION 11 — ACCESS TO CUSTOMER SYSTEMS

11.1 Remote Access Coordination. Supplier-initiated remote access to Customer
systems, whether Interactive Remote Access by a Supplier representative or
system-to-system access by a Supplier service, shall be initiated only through
Customer's Intermediate System, shall be requested in advance against an
approved change record, and shall be subject to Customer's ability to observe
and terminate the session at any time without notice.

11.2 Session Records. Supplier acknowledges that all remote access sessions are
recorded and consents to such recording.

11.3 Onsite Access. Supplier personnel requiring onsite access shall be escorted
at all times unless separately authorized in writing by Customer.

SECTION 14 — TERM AND TERMINATION

14.1 Initial Term. Three (3) years from the Effective Date.

14.4 Transition Assistance. Upon expiry or termination, Supplier shall provide
reasonable transition assistance for a period of ninety (90) days, including
transfer of configuration baselines and documentation necessary for Customer to
operate or replace the supplied equipment.
