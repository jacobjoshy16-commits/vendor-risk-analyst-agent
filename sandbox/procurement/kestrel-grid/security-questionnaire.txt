Q3. Does your organization maintain a documented secure development lifecycle?

A3. Yes. Kestrel maintains a documented secure development lifecycle covering
threat modeling, static analysis, dependency scanning and release signing. The
lifecycle document is available under NDA.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q7. Describe how a customer can verify the integrity and authenticity of
firmware you distribute.

A7. Every firmware release is signed with the Kestrel release signing key. The
signature and a SHA-256 digest are published on the Kestrel support portal
alongside the release. The signing public key and its fingerprint are published
separately in the Kestrel Security Advisory feed, and Kestrel will confirm the
fingerprint by telephone on request.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q9. How are customers notified when a Kestrel representative should no longer
have access to customer systems?

A9. Kestrel maintains an internal offboarding process. Access to customer
systems is managed through the customer's own access request process.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q12. Do you notify customers of security incidents affecting your products?

A12. Yes. Kestrel notifies affected customers of security incidents within 24
hours of confirmation, in accordance with our contractual obligations.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q15. Do you disclose known vulnerabilities in your products?

A15. Yes. Kestrel publishes security advisories for confirmed vulnerabilities
and provides advance notice to customers under active support agreements.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q18. Is a supply chain risk assessment performed on your own suppliers?

A18. Kestrel performs supplier assessments at onboarding. Reassessment cadence
is under review.
