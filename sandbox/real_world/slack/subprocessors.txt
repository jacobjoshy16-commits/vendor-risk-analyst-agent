Salesforce Infrastructure and Sub-processors
Published: July 29, 2026

Slack (a Salesforce Covered Service) engages the following subprocessors to process Customer Data.

Entity | Purpose of Processing | Location of Processing
Amazon Web Services, Inc. | Cloud infrastructure hosting for Slack | United States, Germany, Japan, Australia
Google LLC | Analytics and workspace integrations | United States
Twilio Inc. | SMS delivery for two-factor authentication | United States
OpenAI, L.L.C. | Slack AI summarization and search (when enabled) | United States
Anthropic PBC | Slack AI agent features (when enabled) | United States
Cloudflare, Inc. | Content delivery and edge security | Global
